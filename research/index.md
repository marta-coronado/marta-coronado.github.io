# {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-microscope" %}Research

My research aims to understand **how natural populations adapt to their environment**, and which parts of the genome make this possible.

During my PhD I mapped the action of natural selection across the _Drosophila melanogaster_ development and anatomy following a multi-omics data integration approach, and co-developed methods and resources to estimate adaptation, such as the integrative McDonald and Kreitman test (iMKT) and PopHumanScan.
As a postdoc at the Institute of Evolutionary Biology (IBE, CSIC-UPF), I studied the interplay between **transposable elements, epigenetic marks and gene expression** in natural populations of _D. melanogaster_, and co-led the DEST 2.0 worldwide population genomics resource.

## Current research lines

- **Urban adaptation in disease vectors.** How mosquitoes adapt to cities: transposable elements and recent positive selection in _Anopheles coluzzii_ and _An. gambiae_, and contemporary and historical genomes of _Culex pipiens_ (project [URBANOMICS](projects)).
- **Transposable elements, epigenomics and gene regulation.** The genomic-context-dependent effects of transposable elements on chromatin and on the transcriptomes of different tissues.
- **Genomic resources for biodiversity.** Reference genomes of the tick species present in Catalonia, within the Catalan Biogenome Project (project [CATick](projects)).
- **Population genomics of _Drosophila_.** Worldwide patterns of adaptation, within the European _Drosophila_ Population Genomics Consortium (DrosEU).

**Research interests:** population genomics · natural selection and adaptation · transposable elements · epigenomics · urban evolution · bioinformatics
