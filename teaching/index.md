## Teaching innovation

- **AIR-LAB** (2026–2027): teaching innovation project I lead at the UAB, for the Biostatistics course.
- **Adapta't** (2019–): service-learning project of the BSc in Genetics, where I teach workshops on science communication and infographic design.
- **E-learning platforms** (2019–2020): websites and Moodle spaces for Adapta't, the Genetics permanent classroom, the MSc in Bioinformatics and the UAB Genomics and Bioinformatics Service.

## Courses and workshops

- _Principles of Evolution: Biology through Time and Organizational Levels_, LifeHUB-CSIC, Doñana Biological Station (2023, 2024).
- Introduction to bioinformatics and data visualization in R, CIBEREHD bioinformatics platform (2022–2025).
- Introduction to bioinformatics for virologists, Catalan Society of Biology (2025).
